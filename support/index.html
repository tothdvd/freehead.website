Common questions

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. FreeHead has no account and no server, so nothing is ever sent to us. The one exception is calendar sync: turn it on and your scheduled items are written to your default calendar, which may be an online account like Google or Outlook. Full details in the privacy policy.

Will I lose everything if I delete the app?

Yes — because the data lives only on your device, deleting FreeHead deletes your tasks with it. Your iPhone's own encrypted backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) includes FreeHead's data, so restoring a backup brings it back. iCloud sync across devices is planned.

The app looks too simple / too complicated.

Both are adjustable. Open Settings inside the app and switch between Simple and Complex. Simple gives you an inbox and one list. Complex adds projects, waiting-for, scheduled items, someday and reference — and you can turn each of those on or off individually.

Can I change the language?

Yes, in the app's own Settings, independently of your iPhone's language. FreeHead speaks English, German, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified) and Hungarian.

Notifications or the widget aren't working.

Check iPhone Settings › FreeHead and make sure notifications are allowed. For the Lock Screen widget, touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise, and add FreeHead. If it still misbehaves, email us — include what you expected to happen.

Why does it want full access to my calendar?

So it can keep an event in step with the task it came from. If you move a task to a different day, FreeHead has to find the event it made earlier in order to move it too — and it can only find it by looking. With the lesser "add only" permission it could create events but never correct them, so a changed date would leave a wrong event behind and add a second one next to it.

FreeHead only ever looks up events it created itself. It does not read, search or send anywhere the rest of your calendar. If you would rather not grant it, decline — everything else in the app works, you just lose the calendar mirror.

I deleted something by accident.

Look in Trash. Deleted items stay there for 30 days and can be restored with a swipe.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app — that removes your tasks, notes, projects and people. There is no account to close and nothing for us to erase on our side.

Two things live outside the app and stay behind: any events FreeHead put in your calendar, which you delete in the Calendar app; and older device backups, until they are replaced. Details in the privacy policy.
