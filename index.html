FreeHead

Get it out of your head.

FreeHead is a task manager for iPhone. Capture whatever is on your mind in a second, sort it when you have a moment, and stop carrying it around.

How it works

Everything you capture lands in one inbox. Later, you go through it and decide what each thing actually is — something to do, something you are waiting on someone else for, a bigger piece of work, or something you can let go of.

That is the whole idea. Your mind stops being the place you store things, and becomes the place you think.

What's in it

  • Quick capture that understands dates — type "call the dentist friday"
  • A to-do list that shows what is late, what is waiting, and what is done
  • Projects with progress, for anything that takes more than one step
  • A weekly review that walks you through what needs attention
  • Home screen and Lock Screen widgets
  • Search across everything you have written
  • Six languages: English, German, Spanish, Japanese, Chinese, Hungarian

Your data stays on your iPhone

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. FreeHead has no server of its own, so nothing is ever sent to us. See the privacy policy.
